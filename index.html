Welcome!

I am Marco, a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at the Department of Economics, Ca' Foscari University of Venice, supervised by Prof. Monica Billio and Prof. Dario Palumbo, and a part-time researcher at Fondazione Eni Enrico Mattei (FEEM).

Following an earlier visiting period at the University of Cambridge, I returned as a Visiting Ph.D. Student working with Prof. Andrew Harvey and Prof. Paul Kattuman.

My research lies at the intersection of time-series econometrics, extreme value theory, and environmental statistics, with a particular focus on dynamic score-driven models for climate extremes. I am also currently working on applications to Financial Extremes.

Dynamic Models for Climate Extremes

with Andrew Harvey and Dario Palumbo

Cambridge Working Papers in Economics (No. 2620), Faculty of Economics, University of Cambridge, 2026.

Working Paper (Cambridge Repository)
Abstract

This article proposes a new score-driven time series model for dealing with maxima and minima. A modification, called the composite score, is used to guarantee invertibility. The statistical properties of the maximum likelihood estimator are investigated and applications to river flow and temperature shows that the model works well in practice.
